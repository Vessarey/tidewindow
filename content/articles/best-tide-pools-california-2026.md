**California's tidepool calendar is turning toward afternoon lows. September 27 is a modest, above-zero option at all four covered stations; October 25 brings Good-rated minus-tide windows. The deepest lows with daylight somewhere in their surrounding windows fall on December 24 in our July–December dataset: −1.88 ft at La Jolla (NOAA 9410230) and San Diego (9410170), −1.83 ft at Monterey (9413450), and −1.90 ft at Pillar Point (9414131). Daylight and park hours still decide what those numbers mean.**

*Daylight-window counts and rankings below require at least 30 minutes of daylight overlap, matching the station fact sheets.*

## September 27 versus October 25

The highest-scoring September window at every covered California station is Sunday, September 27. None of those four lows is a minus tide. A month later, Sunday, October 25 gives all four a Good-rated afternoon minus-tide window.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| La Jolla | +0.22 ft, 4:17 PM | 55 · Fair | −0.37 ft, 3:27 PM | 65 · Good |
| San Diego | +0.21 ft, 4:22 PM | 55 · Fair | −0.35 ft, 3:31 PM | 65 · Good |
| Monterey | +0.46 ft, 5:48 PM | 44 · Fair | −0.17 ft, 4:52 PM | 61 · Good |
| Pillar Point | +0.19 ft, 5:57 PM | 52 · Fair | −0.52 ft, 4:59 PM | 68 · Good |

*Computed 2026-09-10 from the four station fact sheets. Heights are feet MLLW; times are local Pacific time. These are selected lows, not complete daily high/low tables.*

September is a lighter-water preview, not a repeat of July's dawn run. At San Diego, September 27's 4:22 PM low falls just eight minutes before Cabrillo's posted tidepool close. A station score does not account for that shortfall. October 25's 3:31 PM low is earlier relative to the same closing time.

## Christmas week: depth is only half the comparison

December 24 has the deepest low in each station's July–December daylight-overlap ranking. The clock tells a more complicated story.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tue, Dec 22 | −1.49 ft, 2:17 PM | −1.49 ft, 2:20 PM | −1.41 ft, 3:31 PM | −1.56 ft, 3:34 PM |
| Wed, Dec 23 | −1.80 ft, 3:02 PM | −1.81 ft, 3:05 PM | −1.73 ft, 4:19 PM | −1.85 ft, 4:22 PM |
| Thu, Dec 24 | −1.88 ft, 3:47 PM | −1.88 ft, 3:50 PM | −1.83 ft, 5:06 PM | −1.90 ft, 5:10 PM |
| Fri, Dec 25 | −1.72 ft, 4:33 PM | −1.71 ft, 4:36 PM | −1.71 ft, 5:53 PM | −1.72 ft, 5:58 PM |

*Computed 2026-09-10 from the station fact sheets. Feet MLLW, local Pacific time. A listed low is not a statement that the site is open or that the low occurs in daylight.*

On December 24 the La Jolla-to-Pillar Point difference is 83 minutes. This is a comparison of this date's predictions, not a fixed coast-wide offset. Monterey's 5:06 PM low and Pillar Point's 5:10 PM low both occur **after sunset**. Their earlier, falling-tide windows overlap daylight for 167 and 157 minutes respectively; those daylight minutes do not extend to the bottom of the tide.

Scores can also favor a shallower holiday window. La Jolla's December 25 low scores 90 (Exceptional), while its deeper December 24 low scores 84 (Great). The [methodology](/methodology/) explains the daylight and weekend/holiday factors. Neither score incorporates a park gate.

## Cabrillo and Fitzgerald: compare the clock with the gate

**Cabrillo:** [NPS posts](https://www.nps.gov/cabr/planyourvisit/hours.htm) a 9 AM–5 PM park day and a 4:30 PM tidepool-area close, with the park closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. The San Diego lows on December 22, 23, and 24 fall before that tidepool close. December 25 is not a Cabrillo option, regardless of its station score. NPS also notes that the tidepool road can close temporarily when parking reaches capacity in its [visit guidance](https://www.nps.gov/cabr/learn/nature/tidepools.htm).

**Fitzgerald:** the [county hours page](https://www.smcgov.org/parks/fitzgerald-marine-reserve-hours) lists an 8 AM opening, including holidays. Closing is 7 PM after Labor Day through October 11, 6 PM October 12–November 1, and 5 PM from November 2 through February 2027. December 23's 4:22 PM low falls 38 minutes before the winter close. December 24's deeper 5:10 PM low falls ten minutes after it. Operating hours can change with conditions; this arithmetic is not access clearance.

The same distinction applies elsewhere. A public shoreline is not automatically an always-open access route. This guide does not certify unrestricted entry at La Jolla, Pacific Grove, or Pillar Point; use the exact location's current official information.
