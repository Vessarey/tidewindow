Predictions are not observations, and the score does not account for surf, closures or route access.

## Where are the access and parking sources?

The [Marine Science Institute's directions](https://www.sfbaymsi.org/directions-to-half-moon-bay-pillar-point), checked October 15, identify West Point Avenue and a parking lot reached by following the road around the marsh. The [San Mateo County Harbor District](https://www.smharbor.com/pillar-point-harbor), which operates Pillar Point Harbor, lists public parking among its facilities. Its 8 AM–5 PM hours are **harbor office hours**, not a verified reef-access schedule.

This page no longer supplies a fixed 20-minute walk or a one-hour-before-low arrival instruction. Those numbers do not account for the route, parking, current restrictions or time needed to return. A low-water prediction does not establish that the shore is open or reachable.

For general tidepool footwear, the [NPS Cabrillo guidance](https://www.nps.gov/cabr/learn/nature/tidepools.htm) says, "Shoes with good gripping soles are best". This is quoted general guidance from a different park, not a Pillar Point access rule.

## Is this the same place as Fitzgerald Marine Reserve?

No. [Fitzgerald Marine Reserve](https://www.smcgov.org/parks/fitzgerald-marine-reserve) is the county park at Moss Beach within Montara State Marine Reserve. The [California Department of Fish and Wildlife](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Montara-Pillar-Point), checked October 15, identifies Montara SMR and Pillar Point SMCA as separate neighboring protected areas. For Montara, it states: "It is unlawful to injure, damage, take, or possess any living, geological, or cultural marine resource." Pillar Point SMCA has separately specified exceptions; the linked state page provides the boundaries and current regulations.

At Fitzgerald, the [county regulations](https://www.smcgov.org/parks/fitzgerald-marine-reserve-regulations) separately prohibit removing shells, rocks and marine life, and pets are not allowed at the tidepools. Its visitor guidance says, "Walk carefully around the tidepools for your own safety and to spare the marine life underfoot". These are Fitzgerald-specific rules and guidance, not proof of access at a neighboring location.

We cover Fitzgerald's dates in a [separate guide](/guides/fitzgerald-marine-reserve-tide-pooling-2026/). Both articles use station 9414131 predictions; that does not make the two places' opening hours, routes or conditions interchangeable.
