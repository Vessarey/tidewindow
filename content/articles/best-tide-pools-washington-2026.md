## What changes after September?

The outer coast and Puget Sound diverge as the daylight shifts. In the October–December 2026 facts:

- **Seattle:** no daylight minus-tide windows. October's two daylight windows have positive lows; November and December have no qualifying daylight windows.
- **Port Townsend:** October 5 is the lone October daylight-minus window, but its −0.23 ft low is at 5:48 AM, before sunrise. Only 39 minutes of the window overlap daylight, and it scores 27, Skip. November and December have no qualifying daylight windows.
- **La Push:** some evening intervals still overlap daylight. Sunday October 25 has a −0.71 ft low at 6:48 PM, 71 minutes of daylight overlap, and a score of 52, Fair. That does not make the entire evening window a daylight outing.

For the inland picture, continue to the [Puget Sound low-tide calendar](/guides/puget-sound-low-tide-calendar-2026/). The [Washington king-tides guide](/guides/king-tides-washington-2027/) covers the separate winter high-tide story; a king-tide date is not a promise of a usable daytime low.

## Access checks before choosing a beach

**Rialto is still inaccessible via Mora Road.** The [NPS conditions page](https://www.nps.gov/olym/planyourvisit/conditions.htm), checked September 8, lists the July 8–October 15 construction closure and says: "Rialto Beach will not be accessible via Mora Road during this construction period." Recheck after the scheduled closure; October 15 is not a confirmed reopening.

**Second Beach has separate access and parking.** NPS lists a [0.7-mile forest-and-beach trail](https://www.nps.gov/olym/planyourvisit/second-beach-trail.htm) and a $10 daily fee for parking on Quileute Tribal Lands, including Second Beach, effective May 1, 2026. Its [tidepooling page](https://www.nps.gov/olym/planyourvisit/tidepool-activities.htm) includes Second Beach among the park's intertidal destinations. This is not a Discover Pass parking arrangement.

**Fort Worden and Constellation Park are different jurisdictions.** [Washington State Parks](https://parks.wa.gov/find-parks/state-parks/fort-worden-historical-state-park) lists Discover Pass parking at Fort Worden. [Seattle Parks](https://www.seattle.gov/parks/parks/charles-richey-sr-viewpoint) places Constellation Park within Charles Richey Sr Viewpoint, along Beach Drive SW at Alki Point. Use those official pages for current destination details.

These are tide *predictions*, not observations. The computed intervals are not access or safety guarantees. The [methodology](/methodology/) explains the threshold, daylight overlap and score; current official park notices remain separate from the tide calculation.
